# Shoe Shop Inventory & Sales Tracker

A simple inventory and sales management application for a small shoe business.

## About the Project

The Shoe Shop Inventory & Sales Tracker is a web application designed to help small shoe business owners manage their products, track available stock and record daily sales.

The goal is to make it easier to monitor inventory and understand how the business is performing.

## Planned Features

- Add and manage shoe products.
- Track available stock.
- Update quantities when new stock arrives.
- Record product sales.
- Automatically update inventory after a sale.
- Get low-stock and out-of-stock notifications.
- View daily sales revenue and gross profit.
- Search and filter products.
